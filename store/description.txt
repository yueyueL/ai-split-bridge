Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok side by side in Chrome’s native Split View. Click Send both to share one prompt with both chats, including links and supported files. You can select more AI tabs or use the usual Send button for one chat.

• Compare answers — See where the answers agree, where they differ, and what needs checking, even when both assistants agree.
• Cross-review — Ask each assistant to critique the other’s answer, then choose one to revise its own.
• Share messages — Share a prompt, reply, or whole exchange with supported files. Highlight a passage to share just the text.
• Summarize a chat — Share a summary so another assistant can continue the conversation.
• Customize actions — Edit the built-in sharing and review prompts, add your own actions like “Check sources,” and set a default.
• Read and navigate — Switch chats on either side, widen messages, collapse replies, and bookmark a message for later.

No extra account, API key, ads, or analytics. The extension runs in your browser and stores no chat transcripts. Files upload to the destination chat even in draft mode.

Requires desktop Chrome 140 or later with Split View. AI Split Bridge is independent of the AI providers.
