Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok in Chrome’s native Split View. Ask two assistants the same question, compare their independent answers, or have each assistant review the other’s work.

• Send both — Type once to send the same prompt, links and supported attachments to both chats. You can also select more AI tabs in the same window. Use Cmd+Shift+Enter on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+Enter on Windows and Linux.

• Compare — Choose one assistant to read both answers. It highlights agreements, disagreements, missing details and claims that need evidence, including claims both assistants make.

• Cross-review — With one click, each assistant critiques the other’s original answer. Choose Find errors or Suggest improvements for a more focused review. Read both critiques side by side, then pick an assistant to revise its answer if you want.

• Create your own actions — Add “Check citations” to Cross-review or “Extract next steps” to the reply menu. Give each action its own prompt, edit the built-in prompts, and choose a one-click default for each menu. Saved instructions can add rules across actions.

• Share to the other side — Click the other assistant’s name beside a reply or one of your own prompts. Supported prompt attachments and generated files go with the whole message. Use the arrow to change chats, adjust instructions, or leave files out. You can also share a highlighted passage or select several messages in order.

• Switch chats — Choose which conversation appears on either side of the split and return to a previous conversation without rearranging your tabs.

• Reading tools — Widen the message column, collapse individual prompts or replies, and bookmark a message to jump back to it later.

The controls sit inside the AI websites you already use. Each site’s regular Send button still sends only to that chat. AI Split Bridge requires no separate account or API key.

Privacy

The extension runs in your browser. The AI providers you choose receive the content you share. Files upload to the destination chat when added to a draft. The extension does not store chat transcripts or send them to the developer. There are no analytics or ads.

Requires Chrome 140 or later with Split View available. Works on chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and grok.com. AI Split Bridge is an independent extension. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
